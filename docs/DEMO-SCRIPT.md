# ArGrid Guided Customer Demo

Recommended duration: 7–10 minutes.

## Before the meeting

1. Open the deployed GitHub Pages URL.
2. Confirm the header shows **LIVE** and **Simulation**.
3. Select **Cikarang Manufacturing**.
4. Select the **Normal operation** scenario.
5. Refresh once so the presentation starts from the deterministic initial state.

## Story 1 — Enterprise visibility

Open **Overview**.

Narrative:

> ArGrid joins live electrical demand, energy cost, reliability, opportunity value and verified savings in one operational context.

Point out:

- Live power and today’s energy.
- Month-to-date financial exposure.
- Verified savings.
- Energy flow from supply to major processes.
- The single contextual insight rather than a wall of alerts.

## Story 2 — Prevent a demand charge

Open **Demand & Cost** or start the guided demo.

Select **Peak demand risk**.

Narrative:

> The platform predicts the end-of-interval value before the charge occurs, identifies the loads creating the risk and quantifies the avoidable cost.

Demonstrate:

- Projected demand crossing the contract line.
- Contributor ranking.
- Deferring one or more noncritical loads.
- Reduced financial exposure in the what-if result.

## Story 3 — Investigate electrically

Open **Electrical Network** and select **Voltage dip**.

Narrative:

> Management impact and engineering root cause remain connected. The operator does not need to rebuild context in another tool.

Demonstrate:

- Feeder F-07 changing state.
- Operations, energy, power-quality and maintenance layers.
- Selecting a feeder and reviewing current measurements and trend.

## Story 4 — Convert insight into work

Open **Opportunities** and select **Efficiency opportunity**.

Narrative:

> An abnormal trend becomes an auditable opportunity with evidence, confidence, probable cause, payback and a recommended verification step.

Open an opportunity and point out:

- Annualized avoidable cost.
- Confidence.
- Baseline evidence.
- Probable cause.
- Responsible owner and next action.

## Story 5 — Prove value

Open **Actions & Savings**.

Narrative:

> The product tracks conversion from detection to verified and persistent saving, so management can see realized value instead of theoretical opportunity.

Show:

- Conversion funnel.
- Action board.
- Baseline versus reporting period.
- Savings ledger and persistence state.

## Story 6 — Close the commercial workflow

Open **Billing** and select **Billing close**.

Narrative:

> Meter quality, tariff calculation, allocation, approval and invoice provenance are controlled in one workflow.

Demonstrate:

- Billing-period stage.
- Validation exception.
- Invoice preview.
- Utility bill discrepancy.
- Calculation trace back to meter readings.

## Closing statement

> ArGrid is not positioned as another energy dashboard. It connects electrical operation, predictive intervention, accountable action, verified savings and auditable billing in one industrial product experience.

Return to **Overview** and summarize:

- Avoided demand exposure.
- Annualized opportunity value.
- Verified savings.
- Critical event detected.
- Billing discrepancy identified.

## Safety statement

All values and actions in this public demonstration are simulated. The demo does not communicate with field devices and cannot issue real electrical commands.
